### Half Adder Demo

Demonstrates the transistor-based half adder producing the correct Sum and Carry outputs.
Two spdt switches control the inputs on the left side of the breadboard. Input LEDs are
displayed on the left-hand side, the red LED on the right is Sum and the white LED is Carry.
