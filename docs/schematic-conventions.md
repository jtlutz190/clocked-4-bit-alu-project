## Schematic Conventions

- 'U1','U2', etc. identify individual ICs.
- Letters such as 'U3A-U3D' identify individual gates within a multi-gate IC.
- Bus notation such as 'A[3:0]' represents four related signals.
- Identically named nets are electrically connected even when no continuous wire is shown.
- VCC and GND represent shared power rails.
- Repeated switch and LED circuits may be shown once and annotated as identical.
- Functional blocks are used for complex subsystems when their internal circuitry is documented separately.
